EMOM 10 – Loopy Timer

EMOM means “every minute on the minute”: work for 45 seconds, rest for 15, and start the next round on the minute. This timer runs 10 rounds.

About Loopy Timer

Loopy Timer is a free interval timer for Tabata, HIIT, EMOM, Pomodoro and your own cycles. Run several timers at the same time, right in your browser. No sign-up, no install.

  • Several timers running at once, each with its own work / rest cycle
  • Presets for Tabata, HIIT, EMOM and Pomodoro, or build your own
  • Sound cues, saved timers and offline use as an installable web app
  • Share your finished workout or focus session as text or an image

FAQ

Is Loopy Timer free?

Yes. It is free to use, needs no account and runs in your browser.

Can I run more than one timer at once?

Yes. Add as many timers as you like and start them independently, for example a Tabata set and a Pomodoro session side by side.

Are my timers saved?

Timers are saved in your browser, so they come back after a reload. Your timers are never uploaded. You can also add the app to your home screen and use it offline.
